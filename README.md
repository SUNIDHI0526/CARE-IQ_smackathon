> **One Secure Health Locker. One Intelligent Assistant. One Life-Saving Platform.**

CAREIQ is an AI-powered digital healthcare platform that centralizes a patient's medical records, simplifies healthcare information, and enables instant access to critical health data during emergencies. By combining secure cloud storage, OCR technology, artificial intelligence, and emergency response features, CAREIQ makes healthcare more accessible, organized, and intelligent.

---

# 🚀 Problem Statement

Medical records are often scattered across paper files, hospital portals, WhatsApp chats, laboratory websites, and physical prescriptions. During emergencies, doctors frequently lack immediate access to a patient's complete medical history, delaying treatment and increasing medical risks.

Additionally:

* 📂 Medical history is fragmented across multiple platforms.
* 🚑 Emergency doctors often cannot access vital patient information instantly.
* 📝 Handwritten prescriptions are difficult to read.
* 💊 Medication errors occur because of unclear prescriptions.
* 📄 Complex medical reports are difficult for patients to understand.

---

# 💡 Our Solution

CAREIQ provides a secure AI-powered Digital Health Locker where patients can store, manage, and access all their medical records from a single platform.

Using Artificial Intelligence, Optical Character Recognition (OCR), and cloud technologies, CAREIQ transforms traditional healthcare records into an intelligent digital ecosystem.

# ✨ Key Features

## 🔒 Centralized Medical Locker

Securely store:

* Prescriptions
* Lab Reports
* X-Rays
* MRI & CT Scans
* Discharge Summaries
* Vaccination Records

## 📄 AI Prescription Reader

Upload or scan handwritten prescriptions.

The OCR engine automatically:

* Extracts handwritten text
* Converts it into digital format
* Organizes prescriptions
* Reduces medication errors

---

## 💊 Smart Pill Identifier

Scan medicine strips or QR codes to instantly view:

* Medicine Name
* Dosage
* Purpose
* Expiry Date
* Side Effects

Automatically create medicine reminders.

---

## 🤖 24×7 AI Medical Assistant

The AI assistant helps users by:

* Explaining medical reports in simple language
* Translating difficult medical terminology
* Answering common healthcare questions
* Checking possible drug interactions

> **Note:** CAREIQ provides health guidance only and does not replace licensed medical professionals.
